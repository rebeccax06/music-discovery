# Sound It Out

**Defining and sharing a personal music vocabulary**  
MIT 6.1040 Software Design · Fall 2026  
Team: Rebecca Xiong, Victor, Sanskriti Singh, Bhadra Rupesh

## Project overview

A listener may ask a friend for something *dreamy* and receive quiet acoustic songs, even though they meant layered synthesizers and echoing vocals. Neither person necessarily misunderstands the word; they associate it with different sounds. Once they finally clarify the request through examples, the explanation is often lost in the conversation and has to be repeated.

**Sound It Out** explores a shareable, revisable **Sound Wall**: a small vocabulary of listener-defined words or feelings, connected to songs that fit, songs that do not, and optional explanations. Its central goal is to help friends understand a *particular person's* musical descriptions, not to replace existing streaming or discovery platforms.

## Scope

The initial design will focus on the human communication loop: express a listener's sound through examples; share the definition with a friend; receive a suggestion; and refine the definition for future use. Automated recommendation algorithms, large-scale music analysis, and full streaming integrations are not prerequisites for that core interaction.
