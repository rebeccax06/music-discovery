**Potential Concepts**

**Associating**

This potential concept gives a user an independent, editable space to define a subjective musical label like "hype" or "dreamy" in their own words. It prevents one person's interpretation from overwriting another's, allowing everyone to maintain their unique personal music vocabulary.

**Excerpting**

This concept isolates a specific timestamped interval of a track so users can reference exact musical moments without having to scrub through the entire song. It prevents identical audio selections from being treated as unrelated, ensuring everyone is talking about the exact same sound byte.

**Annotating**

This concept preserves a user's specific text explanation for why a track or excerpt fits a particular label as an example or counterexample. It prevents tracks from losing their intended meaning and ensures private explanations don't become public just because the label itself is shared.

**Suggesting**

This concept allows friends to propose tracks for a user's specific definition while keeping those proposals in a pending state until the owner reviews them. It prevents unapproved contributions from altering the owner's accepted collection while keeping track of which songs have already been pitched.

**Appraising**

This concept records a listener's private, revisable judgment on how well a track fits their criteria, such as noting a "partial match" if the tempo is right but the vocals are wrong. It prevents subjective evaluations from automatically becoming public examples and allows users to keep their nuanced listening notes private.

**LinkSharing**

This concept generates unique, revocable links that grant read-only access to a specific subset of a user's definitions and shared examples. It prevents accidental disclosure of private annotations and avoids publishing an entire profile when a user only wants to share a single word with friends.
