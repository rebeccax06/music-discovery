# Access

A user [owns an association](view:access.permissions.ownsPitch) when they created it, and only the owner of an association may:

- Add examples to it
- Annotate its examples 
- Review its pitches
- Publicize its responses
- Remove that association

A user [owns a pitch](view:access.permissions.ownsAssociation) when they created it, and only the owner of a pitch may:

- Remove a pitch
- Change a pitch's visibility
